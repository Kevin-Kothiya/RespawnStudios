
# THE INVASION OF VOS

**A 2D Sci-Fi Action-Platformer developed by Respawn Studios**

*Five days to save a civilization. Five days before everything shuts down.*

---

## About the Game

**The Invasion of VOS** is a single-player, 2D side-scrolling action-platformer combining combat, exploration, platforming, and narrative-driven gameplay.

Set on the distant planet VOS, players take control of **Vector**, one of the first robots ever created, as he fights to protect his civilization from a human invasion.

Originally a peaceful society dedicated to art, philosophy, and discovery, VOS becomes a target when humanity discovers **Ferron**, a valuable resource used to produce oxygen from iron oxide.

With his energy system failing and an enemy fleet approaching, Vector must organize a resistance, rescue his people, and confront the invading commander, **General Zagan**, before time runs out.

---

## Story

After exhausting the resources of their war-torn homeworld, humans arrive on VOS seeking the materials necessary for their survival.

Vector initially attempts to negotiate peacefully with the invaders, but General Zagan refuses. Human soldiers begin occupying cities, capturing robots, and exploiting the planet's resources.

During his captivity, Vector discovers two critical facts:

- The humans plan to contact their main invasion fleet in **five days**.
- Vector's malfunctioning energy system has only **five days of power remaining**.

After escaping to a hidden underground refuge, Vector joins the surviving robots and begins organizing a resistance.

With limited time and resources, he must learn to fight, rescue captured robots, uncover the humans' plans, and stop the invasion before VOS is lost.

---

## Gameplay Features

- **2D Side-Scrolling Platforming:** Navigate environments using movement, jumping, and climbing.
- **Combat:** Fight human soldiers using melee weapons, firearms, and grenades.
- **Enemy Encounters:** Face riflemen, melee soldiers, heavy units, and elite forces.
- **Exploration:** Discover resources, hidden areas, and important mission items.
- **Equipment and Upgrades:** Collect materials to improve weapons and defensive equipment.
- **Interactive Environments:** Talk to NPCs, use terminals, open doors, and interact with objects.
- **Inventory Management:** Collect, inspect, equip, and use items throughout the game.
- **Story Progression:** Experience narrative cutscenes and objectives across five stages.
- **Boss Battle:** Confront General Zagan in a final battle for the future of VOS.

> **Development Note:** Some features are currently planned or under development and may change as the project progresses.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop

**Explore → Fight → Collect → Upgrade → Complete Objectives → Progress**

Players explore each stage, overcome platforming challenges, defeat enemies, collect resources, and improve their equipment.

Each completed stage advances the story and brings Vector closer to stopping the human occupation.

## Levels and Objectives

The game follows five main stages, each introducing new challenges and advancing the resistance.

| Stage | Location | Main Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | Underground Refuge | Learn movement and combat, meet survivors, and prepare for the resistance. |
| **Stage 2** | Tunnel Invasion | Defend the underground refuge from advancing human soldiers. |
| **Stage 3** | Prison Rescue | Rescue imprisoned robots and retrieve important blueprints. |
| **Stage 4** | Human Command Centre | Obtain the keycard and security information needed to access the final facility. |
| **Stage 5** | Oxygen Production Facility | Defeat General Zagan and stop the fleet transmission. |

### Stage 1 — Underground Refuge

The tutorial introduces the game's core mechanics through exploration of a hidden robot settlement.

Players will learn to:

- Move, jump, and climb platforms.
- Interact with NPCs and environmental objects.
- Navigate dialogue and conversations.
- Collect resources and manage inventory.
- Use weapons and defeat enemies.
- Complete objectives and progress to the next stage.

### Stage 2 — Tunnel Invasion

Human soldiers discover the underground tunnel network and begin advancing toward the settlement.

Vector must defend the refuge and prevent the invaders from reaching the surviving robots.

### Stage 3 — Prison Rescue

Following the tunnel battle, the resistance attempts to rescue captured robots from a human detention facility.

The mission also provides an opportunity to recover weapons, equipment, and important blueprints.

### Stage 4 — Human Command Centre

Vector infiltrates a human military installation to obtain the keycard and security code required to access the oxygen-production facility.

### Stage 5 — Oxygen Production Facility

Vector reaches the central facility as the human fleet transmission approaches.

With his energy system near failure, he must confront General Zagan and stop the occupation before time runs out.

## Main Characters

### Vector (V-01) — Protagonist

One of the earliest robots created on VOS.

Known for his wisdom and peaceful nature, Vector must become a fighter and resistance leader despite his failing energy system.

### General Zagan — Main Antagonist

Commander of the human invasion.

Zagan believes securing VOS and its resources is necessary for humanity's survival, regardless of the consequences for its robotic inhabitants.

### Radar — Recon / Analyst

A technologically skilled robot who supports the resistance through engineering, analysis, and equipment assistance.

## Visual Style

The Invasion of VOS uses a colourful, science-fiction-inspired **2D pixel-art style**.

The visual direction contrasts the peaceful robotic civilization with the oppressive human occupation.

### Colour Palette

| Colour | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Gold / Yellow | Vector, VOS identity, important selections |
| Cyan / Blue | Robot technology, energy, friendly environments |
| Red | Human military forces, danger, critical warnings |
| Dark Blue / Grey | Industrial environments, underground facilities |
| Green / Orange | Nature, environmental lighting, machinery |

### Environment Design

The game features environments such as:

- Futuristic robotic cities
- Underground settlements and tunnels
- Industrial workshops and factories
- Human military installations
- Detention facilities
- Oxygen-production machinery

The environments become increasingly damaged, industrialized, and militarized as the story progresses.

## User Interface

The interface follows the game's sci-fi pixel-art aesthetic, using dark panels, cyan outlines, gold highlights, and readable typography.

### Gameplay HUD

The HUD is designed to display:

- Player health
- Energy system condition
- Current story day (Day 1/5 through Day 5/5)
- Current objective
- Equipped weapon and ammunition
- Collected resources
- Pause menu access

**Health and energy are separate systems.**

The five-day indicator advances through story progression rather than a continuously running countdown.

### Inventory

Items are organized into five categories:

1. Weapons
2. Consumables
3. Materials
4. Armour
5. Key Items

The inventory allows players to inspect collected items, manage equipment, and access important story-related objects.

### Interactions and Dialogue

Players can interact with NPCs and environmental objects using contextual prompts.

Dialogue panels display character portraits, speaker names, and conversation text.

Pickup notifications confirm collected resources and important discoveries.

## Running the Project

### Requirements

- Windows PC
- A compatible version of GameMaker
- Project files from this repository

### Instructions

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Kevin-Kothiya/RespawnStudios.git
   ```

2. Open **GameMaker**.
3. Select **Open Project**.
4. Navigate to the cloned repository.
5. Open the project's `.yyp` file.
6. Wait for GameMaker to load the resources.
7. Press **F5** to build and run the game.

> The game is currently in development. Some mechanics, levels, and menus may be incomplete.
